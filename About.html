About Sameline Shuttle Service

Airport transfers are what we do best — run from Bedfordview, covering Pretoria, Sandton and the places around them.

Who we are

An airport-transfer specialist

Sameline Shuttle Service specialises in airport transfers. We're based in Bedfordview, close to O.R. Tambo, and we cover Pretoria, Sandton and other places around them.

Bookings are welcome well in advance — a week before is ideal, so we can plan your pick-up properly. Every trip is quoted individually, based on the trip itself and the fuel it will use, so your quote fits your journey.

Beyond the airport, rugby trips are welcome too. Tell us where you're headed and we'll build the trip around it.
